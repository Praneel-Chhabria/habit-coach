A low-friction, high-sustainability daily framework designed specifically for exam holidays, built around your natural 6:00 AM wake-up and 8:00 PM wind-down anchors.

## 2. The 3 Habit Chains (Stacking Frictionless Routines)

ADHD brains struggle with initiating isolated habits. "Habit stacking" pairs hard tasks with actions you already do every single day.

```
[Existing Anchor] ───► [New Habit 1] ───► [New Habit 2]
```

### Chain A: The Morning Hygiene Stack
- **Anchor**: Step into the bathroom right after your run.
- **Step 1**: Shower and wash face with cleanser.
- **Step 2**: Brush teeth immediately while still in the bathroom.
- **Step 3**: Apply moisturizer, followed by sunscreen (the "extra" step).

### Chain B: The Pre-Bed Hygiene Stack
- **Anchor**: Finishing dinner / clock hits 19:30.
- **Step 1**: Head directly to the bathroom before touching the bed.
- **Step 2**: Brush teeth.
- **Step 3**: Cleanse face, apply night moisturizer, and dab treatment/lip balm.
- **Reward**: You get into bed completely clean with zero pending obligations.

### Chain C: The Study Launch Stack
- **Anchor**: Sitting down at your desk at 09:30 with water.
- **Step 1**: Place phone face down in another room.
- **Step 2**: Open only the textbook or notes for today's first topic.
- **Step 3**: Set a timer for 25 minutes (the "only do 25 minutes" trick to bypass startup inertia).

## 3. Study Architecture: Hitting 5–6 Hours Without Burnout

| Block | Timing | Duration | Best Study Type | Mental State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Block 1** | 09:30 – 12:00 | 2.5 hrs | Hardest theory, concept learning, core derivations | Peak dopamine & post-exercise focus |
| **Block 2** | 13:30 – 15:30 | 2.0 hrs | Active recall, solving question sets, past exam papers | Stable afternoon energy |
| **Block 3** | 16:30 – 18:00 | 1.5 hrs | Reviewing mistakes, flashcards, organizing next-day targets | Lower cognitive load / wrap-up |
| **Total** | — | **6.0 hrs** | **Comprehensive Exam Coverage** | **Zero late-night guilt** |

### ADHD Study Rules
1. **Never study without an explicit endpoint**: Break every block into 45–50 min work + 10 min break.
2. **The "Dopamine Parking Lot"**: Keep a blank sheet of paper beside your notes. Whenever random ADHD thoughts pop up ("I should look up this movie actor"), write it on the sheet and return to studying.
3. **Desk Clearance**: Only keep items related to the active subject on your desk. Visual clutter creates cognitive friction.

## 4. Minimal-Effort Cooking Strategies

Cooking twice a day fails when recipes require 10 ingredients and 4 pans. Keep your menu down to low-friction formulas:

### Breakfast Formulas (< 10 Minutes, 1 Pan)
- **Option 1**: Scrambled or fried eggs on toasted whole wheat bread + banana.
- **Option 2**: Oatmeal or muesli cooked with milk, topped with a spoon of peanut butter and sliced fruit.
- **Option 3**: Upma / Poha / ready idli batter (if regional staples are preferred) with boiled eggs.

### Lunch Formulas (< 20 Minutes, Minimal Dishes)
- **Option 1**: One-pot rice & dal / khichdi with mixed frozen veggies (pressure cooker does all the work).
- **Option 2**: Paneer / egg / tofu stir-fry with soy sauce, bell peppers, and roti or steamed rice.
- **Option 3**: Whole wheat wrap or sandwich loaded with paneer/chicken, greens, and yogurt/hummus spread.

## 5. Screen Time Boundaries

- **Morning Shield (06:00 – 09:30)**: No social media or short-form video (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts). Short-form video floods the brain with rapid dopamine, making study sessions feel impossible.
- **Study Mode**: Keep your phone in another room or in a locked drawer. If you need it for lectures, use website blockers for entertainment apps.
- **Night Cap**: Strictly 1 sitcom episode (25–30 min) in bed. Once the credits roll, close the laptop/tablet and open Harry Potter.

---

## 6. The "Emergency Reset" Protocol (When You Inevitably Slip)

With ADHD, bad days will happen. When a morning goes off track:
1. **Do not abandon the day**: If you wake up at 09:00 or skip the run, do not say "today is ruined, I'll start tomorrow."
2. **Jump to the current block**: Look at the clock. If it's 11:00 AM, jump straight into the 11:00 AM schedule.
3. **The 50% Rule**: If 6 hours feels daunting after a rough start, drop your target to 3 hours. 3 hours beats 0 hours every single time.
